Nightfall

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

The short version: Nightfall has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no sign-up. Single-phone mode works fully offline and nothing leaves your device. Multi-device mode (everyone plays on their own phone) sends only what the game needs — your nickname and your moves — to a game room on Google Firebase, and the room is deleted when the game ends. We never sell or share your data.

1. Single-phone mode (offline)

In single-phone mode the app does not connect to the internet. The player names you type and your preferences (sound, language) are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. Uninstalling Nightfall erases them.

2. Multi-device mode (online)

When you create or join a room, Nightfall uses Google Firebase (a service of Google LLC) to connect the phones in the room. Only the following is sent:

  • Your nickname — the name you type to join (up to 16 characters). Other players in the same room see it. You do not need to use your real name.
  • Game data — the room code, your moves (for example votes and night choices), the role the app gives you and connection signals that show whether you are still in the room.
  • A random anonymous ID — Firebase Authentication creates it automatically so the room can tell the phones apart. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or any account.

To keep the game fair, each player's role is visible only to that player and to the room host's phone, which runs the game.

3. How long it is kept

The room — with every nickname and move in it — is deleted when the game ends. If a game is abandoned, the room is removed the next time one of its players opens Nightfall, and nobody can join a room older than 3 hours. The anonymous ID holds no personal information; you can ask us to delete it at any time (see Contact).

4. Security and app verification

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules let each phone read only what it is allowed to see. Firebase App Check uses Google Play Integrity (Android) or Apple App Attest (iOS) to confirm that requests come from the genuine Nightfall app; these checks do not give us any personal information.

5. Where it is processed

The game database is hosted by Google in the European Union (Belgium, europe-west1). Like any internet service, Google processes your device's IP address to deliver and protect the connection. See Firebase privacy and security.

6. What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of data. We do not use the data for anything other than running the game you are playing.

7. Permissions

Nightfall does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. Multi-device mode uses the internet. The app can read text aloud using your device's built-in text-to-speech, which happens locally.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR and Turkey's KVKK you can ask to access or delete data about you. Because rooms are deleted automatically and we hold no names or contact details, there is usually nothing left to delete — but you can always write to us.

9. Children

Nightfall has no ads, no accounts and no tracking. In multi-device mode children should use a nickname rather than their real name.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
